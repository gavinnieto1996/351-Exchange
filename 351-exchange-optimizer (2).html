Share Class Aggregation: Per IRS §351 rules, different share classes of the same company (e.g. GOOG and GOOGL, BRK.A and BRK.B) are treated as a single security. Positions with the same Share Class Group value are combined for the 25% cap test.

§351 Eligibility Rules FLP Atrato
Rules per FLP Atrato Consulting §351 Exchange documentation. Each tax ID must independently satisfy the 25/50 diversification test.
1
No Mutual Funds
Hard Exclusion
Mutual funds cannot be contributed to the §351 exchange. Positions flagged as "Mutual Fund" type are automatically excluded from the optimizer.
2
ETF Look-Through Treatment
Auto-Applied
ETFs contributed receive look-through treatment — e.g., an S&P 500 ETF counts as its underlying 500 securities, not one position. Mark positions as "ETF (look-through)" type. For the 25/50 test, ETFs are treated as diversified baskets and weighted accordingly.
→ ETF positions will not breach the 25% single-position cap unless you enter underlying holdings that do.
3
Top-5 Positions < 50%
Required
The combined value of the 5 largest contributed positions must be less than 50% of the total contribution.

Largest Position < 25%
Required
No single stock, bond, or asset can make up 25% or more of the total contributed portfolio value.

Share Class Aggregation
Auto-Applied
Different share classes (GOOG/GOOGL, BRK.A/BRK.B) are treated as the same security for diversification requirements. Their combined value is tested against the 25% cap. Use the Share Class Group field when entering positions.
6
Prospectus Alignment
Advisory
Contributed securities must be in line with the ETF's prospectus mandate. Mark ineligible positions using the "Eligible" toggle when entering holdings.
